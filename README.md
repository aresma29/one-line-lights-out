A laptop snaps shut mid-sentence and the room goes dark, but the words left unsaid stay glowing in the narrator's hands. This one-liner turns a sudden power-down into a quiet, unfinished goodbye — a small moment of waiting in the dark for an apology that never arrives. The image is deliberately spare: no screen, no light, just a held breath and a sentence that stopped halfway.

> Status: going dark. He closed the lid mid-sentence, so I'll just sit here in the dark, holding his unsent apology.

`main.py` reads the line from `entry.txt` and prints it.
